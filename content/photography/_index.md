I'd consider myself a *very* amateur photographer.  I didn't really get into "practicing" photography until the last 3 or 4 years.  I shoot on a 2012 Sony RX100, and a Sony a6700.  For the a6700, I've got both a Sigma 18-55mm and a Sigma 16-300mm lens.  I tend to enjoy telephoto / zoomed in photos of outdoor and mountainscapes, and general city life.  I'm still working on getting comfortable photographing people, but feel like the candidness of people going about their daily life makes for some of my favorite imagery.
