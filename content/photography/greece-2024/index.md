My partner and I had each done a good bit of traveling around the Greek islands, but had never explored Peloponnesia.  We'd heard it's "where Greek people go on vacation".  We spent two weeks traveling around the area, stopping at roadside tavernas, eating little fried fish and the best Greek salads of our lives, hopping from beach to beach.
