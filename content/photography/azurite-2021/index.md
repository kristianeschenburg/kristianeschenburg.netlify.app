A lot of times, when we're planning routes, we rely on the trip reports of folks who've skied that terrain before.  This time, my friend Ross and I wanted a route that we couldn't find any background information on.  A line where we'd have to piece together 3D mapping information and flyover imagery.  Ross had had his eye on a couloir on Azurite Peak for a while.  Neither of us knew if we it had been skied, or if it could be skied, but we decided to give it a shot.
